Two minutes, once

Your first rule takes one dialog.

Click the toolbar icon, type the domain and the header, and press Save. Chrome asks for access to that one site and nothing else. From then on the rule runs on its own, browser restarts included.

Rules live in local browser storage. They accumulate, they never expire, and JSON export is always free.
